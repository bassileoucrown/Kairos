The thought arrives in the car, in a lift, halfway through somebody else's sentence.

Pad takes one line and asks nothing else of you at that moment. That is the entire point — a capture that demands a category, a date and an owner is a capture you will not make.

Come back later and turn the line into a task, a diary entry or a message.

Private by default. The toggle on each line says, in words, who can read it.
