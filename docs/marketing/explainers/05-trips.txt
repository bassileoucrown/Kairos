A trip is not a flight. It is flights, hotels, the visas it needs, the cars at both ends, and everything that has to move if one leg slips.

Build the trip, add the legs and the stays. Visas are checked against the passports already held. Arrange a car on Movements and it offers to file it here.

When a leg moves, everything downstream of it is re-timed rather than left quietly wrong.
