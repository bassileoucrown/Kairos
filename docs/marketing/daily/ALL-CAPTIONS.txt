── Day 1 · 01-today.png ──────────────────────────────────

Most calendars are built for whoever is doing the booking.

Kairos is built for the person whose time is being spent — and for everyone who protects it.

Open it once in the morning and the whole day is there: what is running now, what is next, and how long you actually have in between.

New from Exousia, in Lagos.

#Kairos #ExecutiveAssistant #ChiefOfStaff #Lagos

── Day 2 · 02-workspace.png ──────────────────────────────

If you run someone's diary, you know the problem. You do the work, and the tool gives you a corner of somebody else's calendar.

Kairos gives you a desk — and it holds every principal you run, not one.

PAs, EAs, Chiefs of Staff: how many logins does your morning currently take?

── Day 3 · 03-household.png ──────────────────────────────

An instruction nobody confirmed is a hope.

The car at 06:15. Dinner for six, two vegetarian. Ask the driver, the chef, the housekeeper — and see, in one place, who has actually said they have it.

The office and the house on the same page, without the house seeing the office.
