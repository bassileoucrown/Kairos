How a meeting reaches the diary

1
Someone asks for time
Through your booking link, or the office adds it
2
The desk decides
Approve, decline, or offer another time
3
It is on the day
On the principal’s phone, in order, with travel time

If you are the principal

You open it once and know the shape of your day.

  • Your day in order, with what is happening now at the top
  • Nobody takes a slot just because they found your link
  • Trips, drivers and travel time, so you leave on time
  • You decide what your office can see

If you run the diary

A desk of your own, not a corner of somebody else’s.

  • Requests arrive in one queue, to approve or decline
  • Briefs, instructions and minutes kept where they belong
  • Draft with help — nothing is ever sent without you
  • Run more than one principal from the same desk
