Pilot Tester Agreement
Version 1.0 · Free pilot · Not a purchase
The Company
Exousia Prime Emporium Ltd, Lagos, Nigeria (“we”, “us”), operator of Kairos by Exousia (“Kairos”).
The Tester
Name · Organisation · Email (“you”).

1 · What this is

You are being given free early access to Kairos, software for running a principal’s diary and the work around it. It is pre-release: parts are unfinished, parts will change, parts will be withdrawn. This covers your use of it during the pilot and nothing else.

2 · What it costs, and what you get

Nothing. There is no billing in Kairos and no subscription is created. Plan tiers exist on paper but are switched off, so you get every feature that works — a tester blocked by a paywall on the thing we asked them to test tells us nothing.

Free access ends when the pilot does. Continuing will then require a paid plan, and we will tell you the price beforehand, not after.

3 · How long

From the date signed below until we close it or you leave. Either of us may end it at any time, for any reason, without notice. Nothing here obliges us to launch, to keep a feature, or to offer you terms later.

4 · Do not make Kairos your only copy

The pilot runs on trial infrastructure that may not be backed up. Data can be lost to a fault, a restart, or a mistake of ours. Keep your own record of anything you cannot afford to lose. We do not guarantee that what you enter will be there tomorrow, and are not liable if it is not.

5 · What you put in, and what we do with it

You decide what goes in. Kairos can hold names, contact details, diaries, trips, correspondence and — where enabled — identity details and documents. We will:

  • use it to run the service for you and to fix faults;
  • keep sensitive fields encrypted where the deployment is configured for it, and record each time one is revealed;
  • not sell it, and not use it to train anything;
  • look at it only where a fault or your own request requires it, and prefer to ask first.

The assistant drafts and reads; it never sends on anyone’s behalf and has no access to the vault.

6 · Other people’s information

Most of what you enter is about other people — your principal, their contacts, their household. You confirm you may enter it and, where the law requires, that those people know. If you are an organisation you remain the controller under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and we act on your instructions. Ask for a data processing agreement and we will sign one.

7 · What we ask of you

A pilot only works if it is used. We ask that you:

  • run real work through it — a diary nobody depends on teaches us nothing;
  • tell us when it is wrong, including when it is merely irritating;
  • give us one short conversation at the end of the pilot.

None of this is a debt you owe us. Stop whenever it stops being worth your time, and say so.

8 · When something breaks

Use Tell us, at the foot of any screen. It sends your name and which screen you were on — never what you have written, and nothing from the vault. Or write to us directly.

We will answer during Lagos working hours and will say honestly whether a thing is being fixed, is waiting behind something else, or is not going to be fixed. There is no support guarantee during a free pilot, and we would rather admit that than imply one.

9 · Please do not

  • use it where a failure would endanger someone, or for anything that must not go wrong;
  • enter card numbers, passwords or bank credentials — it is not built to hold them;
  • attack it or reach for another account’s data. Reporting a weakness you find is welcome and will never be held against you;
  • share your login. Every person needs their own.

10 · Confidence

Kairos is unreleased. Keep screens, prices, features and our plans to yourself until we make them public. You may always say you are testing it, and may always say publicly what you think of it — this protects our plans, not our reputation.

11 · What you tell us

Feedback is the point. Anything you tell us about Kairos we may use, build and keep, without owing you payment or attribution. You keep what is yours — this gives us no rights to your data, documents or business.

12 · No promises about the software

Kairos is provided as it is. We do not promise it is fit for your purpose, that it will be available, or that it is free of faults. We test it hard and it still surprises us. All warranties that may lawfully be excluded are excluded.

13 · Limits of what we owe

You pay nothing, and our liability is limited to the fullest extent the law allows. We are not liable for lost data, lost business, missed meetings or any indirect loss. Nothing here limits liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, for fraud, or for anything that cannot lawfully be limited.

14 · Leaving, and deletion

You may stop at any time and delete your account from inside Kairos. It lists what will go before it goes, and it is irreversible — we cannot restore a deleted account, so export first. On request we will delete what remains within 30 days, except what we must keep by law.

15 · The rest

This is the whole agreement about the pilot and replaces anything said before it. Governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria; the courts of Lagos State have jurisdiction. If a clause is unenforceable the others stand. Neither of us is the other’s partner, agent or employee.
