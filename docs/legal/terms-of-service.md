## 2. What Kairos is

Kairos is a scheduling and custody platform: a shared diary, a booking page,
a place to keep the details that make travel and meetings work, and the tools
for an assistant to run all of it on a principal's behalf.

**Kairos is a tool, not an agent.** It does not make arrangements on your
behalf, does not decide who may reach you, and does not verify the identity of
people who book time with you. What it does is show you what has been asked
and let you or your office answer.

## 4. Principals, assistants, and access

Kairos is built around delegation, so it is worth being precise about it.

**A principal decides who gets access to their account** and what that access
reaches. Access is granted through a pairing code the principal sets from
their own screen, for a window they choose. Scheduling access does not carry
access to identity documents; that is granted separately and deliberately.

**A principal may withdraw access at any time**, and it ends immediately.

**An assistant acts on the principal's instructions.** Where an assistant
publishes an entry, sends a message or holds a document, they do so on the
principal's behalf and the principal remains responsible for it as between you
and us.

**We do not adjudicate between a principal and their assistant.** If a working
relationship ends badly, the principal controls the account and the data in
it; that is the mechanism, and we will not override it on the word of either
side.
