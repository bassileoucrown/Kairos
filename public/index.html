What Kairos actually is

Not a scheduling app that added features. Five integrated systems.

I

Scheduling Infrastructure

The core loop, done properly: onboarding, multi-group management, availability with protected times, tiered meeting types, a public booking flow, calendar, and in-app video — with time zones and reschedules handled correctly from day one.

II

PA Operating Layer

PA Home is a dedicated, first-class interface: an approval queue for sensitive bookings, a brief builder, an instructions vault with voice capture, a communications engine that sends on the principal's behalf, and contact intelligence that remembers every relationship.

III

Document & Asset Infrastructure

For the Family Office tier: a zero-knowledge encrypted document vault, a full family office module for properties, investments, vehicles, and staff, travel itineraries, and expense approval — with a permissions matrix controlling exactly who sees what.

IV

Relationship Intelligence

A three-tier birthday and anniversary system — Inner Circle, Close, Professional — plus an AI scheduling assistant that proposes but never acts alone. Every output waits for the PA's approval.

V

Concierge Marketplace

Coming later

Travel, chauffeurs, private aviation, security, gifting, and more — commercial relationships layered on data the platform already has, once the community that makes those partnerships credible exists.

Trust & security

This will hold passports, property records, and a principal's daily pattern. It is built like it knows that.

Security is not a phase-two cleanup item. It is the foundation the rest of the platform sits on, engineered in from the first line of code.

  • Passkeys & mandatory MFA

    FIDO2 passkeys as the primary identity layer, biometric on mobile, short session windows for the most sensitive tier.

  • Encryption throughout

    AES-256 at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit, with field-level encryption for the most sensitive data.

  • Zero-knowledge document vault

    End-to-end encrypted storage for passports, contracts, and insurance — not even Kairos staff can read your documents.

  • Independently tested

    Third-party penetration testing before any Family Office feature ever touches real user data. No exceptions for speed.
